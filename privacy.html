Privacy policy

Studio by lkdevelopment · last updated 18 November 2026

Who uses this app

Studio is a private tool. The only user is the owner of the YouTube channel Whatifinity, who signs in with their own Google account.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, Studio uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload and schedule videos on the owner's own channel;
  • read statistics (views, watch time, subscribers) of the owner's own channel.

Studio does not access any other Google account and does not collect data about viewers or other people.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
